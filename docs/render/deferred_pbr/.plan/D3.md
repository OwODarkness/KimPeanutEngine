# D3 — Material Asset V2 and PBR G-buffer

Parent: [Deferred PBR plans](../PLANS.md) · Roadmap: [TODO](../TODO.md)

## Design

Material Asset V2 adds `StandardPbr` with explicit base-color, normal,
metallic, roughness, occlusion, and emissive semantics plus color-space
intent. Render resolves assets into pass-compatible material templates and
draws opaque ready proxies through `GBufferPass`.

The G-buffer is three color attachments plus D32: linear albedo RGBA8,
world-space normal RGBA16F, packed metallic/roughness/occlusion RGBA8, and
depth. The imported five-attribute tangent convention is normalized at the
shader boundary and shared by both backends.

## Acceptance

- Material loading/resolution remains Asset → Resource → Render; no native
  material object or GPU handle enters Gameplay.
- G-buffer encoding, texture color-space, tangent basis, and pass
  compatibility are documented and tested.
- Supplied PBR fixtures render through opaque `MeshProxy` draw lists on both
  backends.
