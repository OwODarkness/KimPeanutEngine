# D4 — Directional shadow pass family

Parent: [Deferred PBR plans](../PLANS.md) · Roadmap: [TODO](../TODO.md)

## Design

Render selects at most one compatible enabled `Directional2D` job from the
immutable light snapshot. It records an opaque caster-only depth pass into a
sampled D32 target before the G-buffer, with a Render-private shadow binding
slot and explicit missing/invalid-shadow behavior.

Shadow filtering uses ready opaque casters and a conservative scene-wide fit;
G-buffer camera culling remains independent. Shadow debug output is converted
to a color view by Render rather than exposing raw depth readback.

## Acceptance

- Vulkan and OpenGL produce, sample, capture, resize, and tear down the real
  sampled directional depth target.
- Invalid or absent jobs record no shadow work and resolve safely to clear depth
  or unshadowed lighting.
