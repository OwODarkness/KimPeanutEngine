# C4 — PNG export and visual smoke evidence

Parent: [Render Capture plans](../PLANS.md) · Roadmap: [TODO](../TODO.md)

`RuntimeScreenshotService` validates `save/screenshots/` containment, chooses
deterministic explicit paths for tests, delegates PNG encoding to ImageIO, and
reports failures without success claims. GraphicsSmoke and live runtime
captures provide visual evidence beyond file-signature checks.

Acceptance: Vulkan/OpenGL captures have valid dimensions/signatures, non-empty
pixels, stable orientation, and inspected final artifacts after resize.

Status: complete for RGBA8 PNG; HDR/EXR and image-difference baselines remain
deferred.
