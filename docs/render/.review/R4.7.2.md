**2026-09-27. Runtime blocker corrected in the working tree; optimization acceptance remains open.**

Scope: HEAD `3fd9108` plus the existing uncommitted R4.7.1/R4.7.2 shader,
binding, fallback and queue changes. Preserve those changes. This follow-up
addresses the Cornell access violation and the invalid visibility SBT, rather

## Confirmed findings and correction

### P1 — Visibility miss index addresses the hit group

**Corrected.** `VulkanAccelerationStructureOwner::CreateRayTracingPipeline`
copies records at a 64-byte stride. With two miss shaders, the reviewed
working tree incorrectly set both miss-region stride and size to 128 bytes.
`TraceVisibility` selects miss index 1. Its address therefore advanced past
the visibility record and into the closest-hit record, outside the miss
region. The supplied 11.40.24 log records `miss=(...,128,128)`, confirming
that the erroneous layout reached the executable.

The [Vulkan miss-record rule](https://docs.vulkan.org/spec/latest/chapters/raytracing.html)
uses region base plus record stride times miss index. Region size is the total
extent, not the record stride. The corrected region is **stride 64, size 128**.
This is invalid GPU execution data, which queue locking cannot repair; an
upload queue reporting device loss is an observation point, not proof that
the upload command caused it.

Added a backend-local checked region-layout helper and used it for raygen,
miss and hit regions. Regression coverage verifies one/two miss records,
visibility index 1 selecting the second miss rather than the hit record,
adjacent region bounds, and invalid counts. No common RHI API or shader sample
budget changed in this follow-up.

### P1 — Failed frame cleanup re-enters with a null recorder

**Corrected; fault-injected cleanup retest remains unperformed.** CDB reproduced
the access violation on the pre-fix RelWithDebInfo executable. The stack is:

```text
VulkanCommandRecorder::EndRenderTarget (this == nullptr)
VulkanBackend::EndFrame
RenderSystem::CleanupOwnedState / Shutdown
RuntimeContext::Clear
Engine::RenderThreadFunc exception cleanup
```

`EndFrame` destroys `command_recorder_` before finishing/submitting/presenting,
but previously cleared `frame_active_` only after all operations succeeded.
An exception in that interval left the backend active without a recorder.
Render cleanup called `EndFrame` again and dereferenced null, obscuring the
original frame failure before its late exception log could be written.

Now the backend consumes the active frame before resetting its recorder.
Cleanup sees the already-consumed frame and does not submit/end it again.
Submission serial retirement and resource ownership remain Graphics-owned.
This correction does not provide device-loss recovery or retry failed work.

The stack is saved under
`save/diagnostics/r472/cornell-before-local-stack.txt`. A dump-writing command
failed because the debugger interpreted its backslash path; no successful
crash dump is claimed. The local-symbol stack was sufficient to identify the
null receiver and teardown call chain.

### Startup zero records are not the crash location

**Explained.** Renderer promotion precedes `FinalizeGameStartup` scene
instantiation. The first prepared snapshot can be empty; existing graph gating
then leaves PT inactive until a TLAS and scene records exist. The message is
not evidence that an empty RT dispatch occurred. After the SBT correction,
Cornell subsequently reports **8 geometry / 1 instance**, and Sponza
**450 geometry / 3 instances**, with active PT and advancing samples. No
scene-loader or level-specific workaround was necessary.

the authored fixture camera, 1094×619, 4 SPP and eight continuation bounces.

  active PT and 2,656 samples. Exported and inspected
  `save/screenshots/validation/r472-fix-debug-sponza-beauty-later.png`: textured
  curtains/architecture, direct sunlight and visible indirect interior light.
- Logs `...11.46.27.txt` and `...11.51.26.txt` under
  `save/logs/2026-09-27/` show corrected `miss=(...,64,128)` and no matched
  validation errors, invalid texture/sampler lookups or device-loss errors in
  the inspected runs. Debug timings are correctness diagnostics only.

A preliminary Sponza launch overlapping an attempted Cornell close was aborted
and excluded. `CloseMainWindow` did not reliably terminate the owned runs;
processes were stopped after evidence collection. These runs therefore do not
establish graceful shutdown acceptance. Debugger attachment during Sponza
startup is also excluded from performance evidence.

## Validation and remaining gates

Final Debug and RelWithDebInfo engine builds passed. The Debug
`GraphicsContractTest.exe` ran **30 tests, all passed**, including visibility
miss addressing. The wrapper's `test GraphicsContractTest` regex found no
CTest entries, so the executable was run directly; an empty CTest selection
is not counted as test evidence.

Two corrected RelWithDebInfo Vulkan Sponza windows completed with validation
disabled, mailbox, authored camera `(10.80743, 1.59222, 0)`, 1094×619,
4 SPP / 8 bounces, PT active, ray-query shadows inactive, and complete tracked
texture residency. Each used 120 warm-up and 300 sampled frames. PT GPU p50/p95
was **24.971/26.611 ms** in window 1 and **24.537/27.121 ms** in window 2;
total GPU p50 was **29.037/28.609 ms**, and CPU total p50 was
**28.885/28.666 ms**. The two-window PT p50 median is **24.754 ms** (range
24.537–24.971), about **28.3% below** the earlier three-window median of
34.548 ms. This covers the combined existing R4.7.1/R4.7.2 and corrective
changes, not an isolated R4.7.2 speedup. More windows are needed for a stable
repeated result. No build/test overlapped sampling; no debugger was attached.

Window 1 stats and image are retained in
`save/diagnostics/r472/sponza-rel-stats.json` and
`save/screenshots/validation/r472-fix-rel-sponza-beauty.png`; its log is

The second run reported Vulkan on an RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, PT active, 450

Build/test commands and final results are recorded in the

The observed device-loss/black-image blocker no longer reproduced in the

proving every historical device-loss occurrence had that cause.

Keep R4.7.2 unchecked until matched RelWithDebInfo repeated windows, equal-sample
before/after quality comparisons, shader register pressure and the remaining
transform/probe/occlusion coverage are complete. A valid Beauty image closes
the specific blank-image blocker, not every optimization acceptance gate.
Do not introduce a one-bounce workaround or report Debug FPS as performance.

The pre-existing queue mutex changes are retained. Their allocation/free
locks alone do not prove thread-safe recording from a shared command pool:
[Vulkan command-pool synchronization](https://docs.vulkan.org/refpages/latest/refpages/source/VkCommandPool.html)
also covers recording. This review does not establish concurrent GPU upload
callers or redesign that ownership; assess it separately if such callers exist.
