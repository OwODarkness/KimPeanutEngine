## Landed closeout

The V1 ledger is reconciled in [TODO.md](../TODO.md) and
[live2d-v1-rendering.md](../../../.spec/specs/live2d-v1-rendering.md):

- Items 8, 9, and 10 pass with evidence from L2D5.1–L2D5.3.
- Item 11 is an explicit accepted limitation: the official R5 sample cannot
  be built because GLEW 2.2.0 is unavailable in this environment.
- Hiyori at 1024x768 retains the reproduced 81-pixel, 1/255 tolerance
  overshoot. Mao remains a characterization fixture until its deterministic
  multi-channel mask divergence is fixed.
- The `model/rock1-bl/rock2` content gap remains deferred as unrelated to Live2D.

The landed viewer is the `IApplicationHost` mode `live2d-viewer` of
`KimPeanutEngine.exe`, not a separate `KimPeanutLive2DViewer` binary.
The capture role is `HostOutput`; `capture-view live2d` remains a
compatibility alias.

The L2D6 entry gate is satisfied through update, snapshot, generic submission,
presentation, capture, resize, and clean shutdown. Product bytes, instance
lifetime, and the L2D4 submission boundary are unchanged.
