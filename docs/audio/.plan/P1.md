# P1 — Compact cyberspace music player

Status: proposed. Parent: [audio roadmap](../TODO.md). Dependencies:
[A1](A1.md); [C1](C1.md) for the integrated speech demo.

## Surface and ownership

Build a compact ImGui panel as an Editor UI component. It consumes copied
Audio/session state and issues handle-based commands. Audio owns decoded
track buffers, voice lifetimes, bus gain, playhead, and levels. Asset owns
local-file identity/loading. Editor owns selection and appearance. The panel
must not read the mixer buffer or retain an `AudioPlayer*`.

Initial scope is one local, seekable track with title/source, play/pause,
elapsed and total duration, seek bar, volume/mute, simple bounded level bars,
and a speech status indicator. Present disabled seek/unknown duration
honestly. Music and speech use independent controls. While speech is actually
audible, ramp music gain to a configurable duck level; on silence, pause,
cancel, or completion, recover smoothly. A user music mute/volume choice is
preserved through ducking. Ensure long titles truncate, controls remain
keyboard operable, focus is visible, and 100–200% UI scaling is legible.

Visual direction: a small dark panel with restrained cyan/violet highlights,
thin progress and level tracks, and clear active/error/buffering states. Avoid
decorative animation driven from the audio callback. Any spectrum effect is
deferred until a bounded, downsampled telemetry path is measured.

## Acceptance and validation

- [ ] Local playback, pause, seek, volume/mute, end, reload, and device
  shutdown work without stale handles or audible clicks.
- [ ] Speech ducking starts on audible speech, recovers after a measured ramp,
  and never changes the user's stored music gain.
- [ ] Visual review at compact/expanded widths and 100–200% scaling shows
  readable labels, focus, disabled controls, and accurate progress/levels.
- [ ] Editor target and focused audio tests pass; audible operation and UI
  screenshots are captured through the approved visible engine path.

No playlist database, remote stream, or persistence is required for P1.
