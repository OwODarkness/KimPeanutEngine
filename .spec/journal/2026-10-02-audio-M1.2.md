# Audio M1.2 — archive, reimport, and status

## Outcome

Implemented Audio as an archive product and added source/subtitle dependency
tracking, recorded import options, no-op probing, reimport controls, integrity
checks, and AssetTool status reporting. Archive schema v2 stores Audio-specific
metadata in `source_audio_metadata`; a v1 archive remains readable and upgrades
on a read-write open without changing its existing Model records.

Import hashes the source and optional subtitle, verifies path containment,
validates the cooked product, and avoids decoding or rewriting an unchanged
product. Reimport reuses the recorded subtitle unless explicitly replaced or
cleared. The product is atomically published before the archive source record
transaction; injected transaction failure leaves the prior Ready association
intact. Audio products are recognized by the archive and content catalogs.

## Remaining M1 work

M1.3 still needs the native runtime loader, seekable bounded decode worker,
source lifetime pinning, playback-clock cue query, and long-track memory and
lifetime evidence. M1.4 still needs Editor integration for native imports and
subtitles. OGG remains unsupported in the current decoder build; SRT, WebVTT,
and LRC are accepted subtitle formats.
