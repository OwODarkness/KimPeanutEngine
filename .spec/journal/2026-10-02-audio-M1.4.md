# Audio M1.4 — standalone player migration

Date: 2026-10-02

## Outcome

Standalone Audio Player imports now use `AssetImport::AudioImportService`, load
the generated `.audio` product through `AssetManager`, and prepare a
`SeekableAudioPlayer` with its source lifetime pin. Per-source session archives
live under `save/audio_player/`, so arbitrary music files remain session
previews rather than project Asset registrations. Optional subtitle files can
be chosen explicitly in the Library and selected tracks can be reimported.
The Now Playing dock draws active subtitle text in a larger borderless area
below the waveform. `audio.import_file` accepts an optional `subtitle` path, and
`audio.status` exposes subtitle cue state.

Playback cue lookup follows the player's consumed source-frame cursor. The view
keeps the old active voice's track snapshot while a reimport publishes a newer
product, and cue lookup checks selection before resolving its track. The UI
inspector reports native file-backed playback and subtitle language/cue count.

Follow-up UI fixes enlarge subtitle text, remove its frame, and align the scope
and waveform at the top of their row. RMS, peak, and FFT now read a bounded,
nonblocking snapshot from the seekable decode cache, so native music animates
the circular scope and Spectrum dock without touching callback-owned memory.

## Remaining checks and limitations

- The selected-track reimport path is present, and the active voice retains its
  product reference, but the reimport-during-playback swap has not been smoke
  tested in the running UI.
- Device stop/restart during active playback has not been smoke tested.
- Visible playback on the user's `Default` desktop remains unverified because
  the available shell runs on the isolated Codex sandbox desktop.
- The standalone player uses session-scoped products. Moving an external track
  into the durable project Asset library remains a separate product workflow.
- OGG remains excluded from verified import support until a Vorbis decode
  fixture passes.
