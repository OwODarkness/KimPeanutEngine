# Live2D L2D5.4 — V1 Closeout and L2D6 Handoff

- Status: complete
- Date: 2026-09-12
- Plan: [L2D5.4](../../docs/live2d/.plan/L2D5.4.md)
- Spec: [live2d-v1-rendering](../specs/live2d-v1-rendering.md)

## Result

The V1 ledger is closed with explicit disposition for every item. Items 8, 9,
and 10 pass from the L2D5.1–L2D5.3 evidence. Item 11 is an accepted limitation:
the official R5 sample cannot be built because GLEW 2.2.0 is unavailable, and
cross-backend agreement is not used as a substitute.

The viewer is the live2d-viewer IApplicationHost mode of KimPeanutEngine.exe;
there is no separate viewer binary. The public capture role is HostOutput, with
capture-view live2d retained as a compatibility CLI alias. L2D5 does not change
product bytes, model-instance lifetime, or the generic L2D4 submission boundary.

## Accepted residuals

- Hiyori at 1024x768 reproduces the existing 81-pixel, max-3 (1/255)
  non-edge-opaque tolerance overshoot.
- Mao remains unsuitable as a cross-backend image fixture: its deterministic
  0.40% face-localized RGB divergence is consistent with a multi-channel mask
  defect and needs a separate backend investigation.
- The model/rock1-bl/rock2 content gap remains deferred as unrelated to Live2D,
  per the L2D5.0 decision.

## Evidence

- L2D5.1 proves the HostOutput capture rename and generic Render boundary.
- L2D5.2 proves resize, capture, cleanup, and GPU-handle shutdown assertions.
- L2D5.3 proves Mao blend-mode coverage, records the official-reference blocker,
  and characterizes both Hiyori's resized residual and Mao's divergence.
- The L2D6 entry gate is satisfied through update, snapshot, submission,
  presentation, capture, resize, and clean shutdown.
