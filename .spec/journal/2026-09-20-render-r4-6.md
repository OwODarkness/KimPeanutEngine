## Diagnostic review correction — 2026-09-20

The formal [R4.6 review](../../docs/render/.review/R4.6.md) identified a
concrete API-contract defect in the common failing path. At
`vulkan_acceleration_structure_owner.cpp:582-583`, `vkCmdTraceRaysKHR` receives
`nullptr` for `pCallableShaderBindingTable`. Vulkan requires a valid pointer to
a `VkStridedDeviceAddressRegionKHR`; a pipeline with no callable shader passes
the address of a zero-initialized region.

This finding explains why both the closest-hit and raygen/no-TLAS probes can
fail at the same native command, independently of Cornell geometry and hit
shading. It is the leading root-cause hypothesis, not a verified fix: no source
change or corrected runtime run was made as part of this documentation review.
The SBT limit checks and crash evidence are also incomplete, so a successful
1×1 sentinel dispatch remains the first resume gate.

R4.6 remains blocked. No RT image, reference comparison, or completion claim is
added, and no commit was created.
